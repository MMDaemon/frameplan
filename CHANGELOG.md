# Changelog

The newest version first. Each release's notes are written by `tools.release`.

## 2026.11.27.573 (2026-11-27)

### A new group: Activities
- The menu has a group **Activities** right after the Overview: **Daily & Weekly** (the former Activities page), **Rotations** and **Events**. The side panel's tab is called Activities too.
- **Rotations** now also shows what rotates without the game's live feed saying so: Marie's shop (her pools, until her reset), this week's Steel Path Circuit adapters (which ones are worth it for you), the Archon Hunt, Palladino's weekly offers, Nora's Cred offerings and Acrithis' daily stock (how often each Arcane comes). Each tile leads to its detailed page.
- Tiles on Daily & Weekly, Rotations and Events show their likeliest rewards as small tiles, what you need first.
- The side panel and Right now lead to the matching Rotations tile; the Archon Hunt, Focus and the Circuit lead to their pages; a bounty to its island; a syndicate's details list its bounties now; relic tiles say the fissure open now for their tier.

### Pictures
- DE's own icons first, and pictures DE changes are fetched again.
- A blueprint shows the item it builds, with a small blueprint mark.
- Syndicates show the sigil of your rank; Focus Ways show their icons.

### Fixed
- The Zid-An Arcanes are found again: they have their own chip "Tektolyst Artifacts", and the search finds "zidan" and "tektolyst" (every search now ignores hyphens and apostrophes).

## 2026.11.27.567 (2026-11-27)

### New pages: Rotations and Events
- **Rotations**: everything the game rotates right now, in detail -- the bounty boards of Konzu, Eudico and Mother with every stage's rewards and their chances, Void Fissures, Teshin's offer (this week and next), Baro Ki'Teer (here, or when he comes), Varzia, invasions, the Circuit's Warframes this week, the open worlds' cycles, the Arbitration and the Sentient Anomaly. What you still need is marked on every tile.
- **Events**: what runs now (events, alerts, the Fomorian and the Razorback), what comes up (alerts not started yet, a Fomorian or Razorback still being built, with its progress) and the events that come back while they don't run (Ghoul Purge, Thermia Fractures, Plague Star, Dog Days, Star Days, Nights of Naberus, Tennobaum) -- how often they return in the wiki's words and what they give. No guessed dates: the game doesn't announce them ahead.

### Bounties
- Every drop place of a bounty on Konzu's, Eudico's or Mother's board now says when that reward table is on the board ("Bounty board now · ends in …", or the next board when the game lists it). The Zariman's, the Sanctum's and Höllvania's boards don't name their rotation, so their places aren't marked.
- Right now suggests a running bounty with a reward you need (and that you can take: area open, Mastery Rank high enough) under Next missions.
- The Profit-Taker heist's stages are locked in their first-run order (rank 5 with Solaris United, then stage by stage), like every other lock.

### Better
- What you need is worked out the same way for every live offer: Baro's Primed mods you don't own are now marked (they still don't trigger a notification), and the Circuit's Warframes show again.
- Notifications use the new list: an offer that was already announced is announced once more after this update.

## 2026.11.26.565 (2026-11-26)

- **Goals per page**: owning everything is always the goal; "maxed" and page extras (mod tuning ranks, Focus Tauron Strikes) are optional. A new **Goals** tab in Settings holds every page's goal, the Helminth choice and a reminder switch per activity. Status groups stay the same whatever goal you pick.
- **Archon Shards**: only Tauforged shards are collected (as many as possible); normal shards have no target.
- **Supplies** with a blueprint have two targets: the finished item and its blueprints.
- **Right now** in two parts: first what needs no mission, then **Next missions** (at most three): your next quests, junctions and Star Chart nodes while those are open, then a relic with an open fissure, a quest's rewards or the quickest island.
- **Live offers**: alerts, events, invasions, Teshin's Steel Path offer (this and next week), the Fomorian and Razorback while they run, the Sentient Anomaly's node and the Arbitration. Choose per source in Settings > Pages: always, only when you need a reward, or never. "Need" now also counts resources you are short of and supplies below their target.
- **Activities tick themselves** from your save: Maroo's Ayatan hunt and Clem's weekly mission. The **1999 Calendar** shows days done, the next task or prize choice and which prize you need.

## 2026.11.19.514 (2026-11-19)

- **No story spoilers.** Places and items the story hasn't shown you yet stay hidden until you finish their quest, just like in the game. Quest names still show, as in your Codex. Lists that leave something out end with "More shows up later in the story". You can switch this off: Settings → Pages → "Show story spoilers".
- **Which refinement to open a relic at.** Relic tiles and details now say "Open Intact" or "Open Radiant" for the parts you still need, with the chance per opening. If Radiant is better but you lack the Void Traces, it says so.
- **No more cut-off texts.** Tile texts no longer end in "…": long ones are said shorter on the tile, and the details still give them in full. Names in the small part tiles take up to two lines ("BP" stands for Blueprint).
- **Surplus: one window per Prime item.** Opening a part or a set now shows everything for the whole item: which set you keep, what to sell as whole sets, and every spare part for Platinum or Ducats. Each row says what one copy brings, the total and why, with the sums at the end.
- **Journey: Railjack on its own.** The Proxima regions have their own "Railjack" filter and count; "Star Chart" now means the Star Chart only.
- **Fix:** Perigale, Voruna and other Zariman items now list all the resources their blueprint needs (Lua Thrax Plasm and Thrax Plasm were missing).

## 2026.11.19.508 (2026-11-19)

## 2026.11.19.505 (2026-11-19)

- **Sell or dissolve, your way:** the Surplus page now asks how much Endo and how much Vosfor one Platinum is worth to you. Spare mods and Arcanes are sold only when that brings more than dissolving them. The start values come from the market (a filled Ayatan Anasa for Endo, your own spare Arcanes for Vosfor); a value you enter wins.
- **Arcanes, best way per copy:** sell spare copies one by one, fuse them to max rank and sell, or dissolve them at Loid — whichever brings most.
- **Prime sets:** switch "Prime parts as" to Sets: each set's price against its parts', its Ducats at your rate, and the best way to give it away.
- **Clearer Surplus groups:** every group says what you get ("Dissolve for Endo", "Dissolve for Vosfor", "Sell set for Platinum" …), and each chip shows only its own settings.
- **Why this pack:** Arcane collections are ranked by the farm time a pack saves per 100 of its currency; the recommended one is marked, with the reason.
- **Safer data:** if a day's game data lacks the ranks or drop places of many Arcanes, the page says so instead of quietly showing fewer.
