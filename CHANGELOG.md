# Changelog

The newest version first. Each release's notes are written by `tools.release`.

## 2026.11.18.500 (2026-11-18)

- **Locked things are greyed out, with what opens them.** Pages, parts of a page, islands, planets and items you can't get yet now show greyed out with the steps that unlock them, instead of being hidden. Greyed-out items still count in your goals. Settings → Pages lets you hide them or show them as any other.
- **Surplus is always open.** Each section says what it needs: selling (Mastery Rank 2 and Two-Factor Authentication), the Helminth (its segment), Subsume (Metamorphosis rank 1), Palladino (Chains of Harrow), Arcanes to Vosfor (Whispers in the Walls).
- **Journey: planet details.** Click a planet to see every node with its Star Chart and Steel Path state, the Mastery a clear still gives (or that it only counts for the record), and which nodes Teshin asks for before the Steel Path. Junctions list their tasks; a planet not reached yet says how to get there. The Steel Path stays greyed out until you unlock it.
- **Islands:** chips for Items, Resources, Mods and Arcanes; the Dark Refractory is one island with The Descendia and The Perita Rebellion; shops are places of their own, their items as tiles and the currency in the place's head (thanks for the feedback!); Railjack places are named by their Proxima.
- Netracells are no longer suggested before Whispers in the Walls.
- The Arcane pack advice keeps the currency your missing items still need, leaves out skipped Arcanes right away and says how often a pack is in stock.
- The design (dark, light, like the browser) is now kept per device.

## 2026.11.18.497 (2026-11-18)

Fixes from your feedback:

- **Journey / Steel Path:** Descendia missions done on the Steel Path now count as done. Sanctuary Onslaught, Testudo, The Index and the Railjack confrontations are no longer listed as nodes to do (they aren't Star Chart nodes); Ascension (Brutus) stays.
- **Dark Refractory:** shows its six real missions, each with its mode — The Descendia (Descend, Roathe's Oblivion) and The Perita Rebellion (three Recalls, The Guilty). The Sanctuary checkpoints are no longer counted as missions of their own.
- **Railjack:** Fenton's Field, Profit Margin and the other Proxima missions are now under Railjack instead of the Star Chart.
- **Cephalon Simaris:** his offer of a Warframe's main blueprint (e.g. Nidus) is no longer shown as a source for its Systems, Chassis or Neuroptics.
- **Resources shown as parts:** resources like Dull Button, fish parts or plants were listed as a missing part while you had none; they are resources now.
- **Arcane fusion:** "Right now" has a new group "Arcanes: fuse now" (before buying) that shows the rank the copies you have reach — e.g. rank 0 → 2 with 7 copies — and what to dismantle first. The Arcane's details say so too.
- **Search Pulses:** Temporal and Deep Archimedea can always be ticked off, also when no Arcane you track needs them (their main reward is Archon Shards). Archimedeas you haven't unlocked yet take no Pulses.

## 2026.11.15.491 (2026-11-15)

- **Surplus** (was Trading, now under Progression): everything you hold beyond your plan, in one place, by what to do with it: sell as a set or for Platinum, Ducats at Baro, feed the Helminth (its secretions shown), dissolve mods for Endo and Arcanes for Vosfor, subsume Warframes, open relics for Ducats, Riven Slivers at Palladino. Each section says what it brings altogether.
- Mod, Arcane and resource details say how many are spare, with a link to Surplus.
- Right now suggests the Arcane collection that saves the most farming, with what it costs and brings on average.
- **Journey** is a page of its own (first under Progression) instead of a tab on Islands.

## 2026.11.15.487 (2026-11-15)

- Settings: in tabs (Pages, Appearance, The program, Feedback).
- Appearance: choose Dark, Light or Like the browser (the default). The choice applies at once and stays after a reload.

## 2026.11.15.484 (2026-11-15)

- Details open on top of each other: a click on an item, mod, Arcane, resource, relic, island and so on opens its details over the current view, and you stay on the page you were on. Close, Esc or Back (the back gesture on a phone) closes only the top view. From the second view on, "Close all" takes you back to the page. A click beside a view closes it; with several open, you choose between this one and all.
- Every details view has the same width. An island's place navigation sits as its own panel beside the view, and choosing a place opens that place alone.
- Supplies: a tile opens its details, with the blueprint, the build, other blueprints, vendors and drops. The target is set there now.
- Syndicates: a link opens the syndicate's details.
- Activities: the Nightwave group says what its Standing is for. Islands: a locked tile is as tall as the others.

## 2026.11.15.479 (2026-11-15)

- Islands on a phone: the place tiles in an island's details now fit the screen. Before, a second column was cut off at the right edge, and the details flickered while scrolling.

## 2026.11.15.476 (2026-11-15)

- Incarnon: a tile opens its details. They show the adapter's cost, where you install it and which Circuit week offers it, plus every evolution with the challenge that unlocks it and its perks, with your variants' values. The evolutions come from the wiki.
- Activities: tiles grouped into "Still to do" and "Done", with Daily, Weekly and Nightwave inside. A tile opens its details: what it's for, the Arcanes you track from it, and this week's Circuit adapters. Activities the game doesn't report are ticked off there.
- Trading: groups by what to do, each with what its parts bring altogether, and the categories inside. A tile opens the part's details: what to do with each copy, what you keep and why, its set. The "Choose" boxes are gone.
- Mastery: "Subsumed" instead of "Fed to Helminth", and "Prime owned" when you have the Prime. Each item shows its subtype (Rifle, Bow …) instead of repeating the category it is listed under. An item to level opens its details.
- Tiles: Arcanes and resources show their best place; resources also show what they are for. Relics no longer show "Farmable" or a time.
- Islands: item tiles also show what buys an item, with what you have on hand.

## 2026.11.15.471 (2026-11-15)

- Islands: larger islands now show their areas as places, each with its own unlock. Examples: Isleweaver in Duviri, the Eidolon Hunts on the Plains, the Deepmines in Orb Vallis.
- Islands: the faction syndicates are one island, "Syndicates", with a place per syndicate. Items are grouped by the rank they are bought at, and your current rank is shown.
- Islands: new "Relays" island for Teshin, Arbitration Honors, Zorba, Ergo Glast, Simaris and Baro.
- Islands: new "Clan Dojo" island with one place per lab. It is listed under "No farming needed".
- Island details: the head stays in view while you scroll. Big islands get a place navigation with "Open all" and "Close all", and places can be collapsed.
- Island details: what you are missing at a place is shown as tiles, each with its best drop place and time. A click opens the details.
- Items: anything with every part in hand but resources short is in its own group, "Resources missing".
- Journey: closed areas of open islands are listed again under "Places not available yet".

## 2026.11.14.465 (2026-11-14)

- Updating from inside the app now starts Frameplan again afterwards (it could fail with "Failed to load Python DLL"; if that happened, just start Frameplan from the Start menu).
- Feedback can say what it is about: pick the page and an entry, or use "Report a problem with this" in any details window (shown while you're signed in to GitHub). The entry's data is attached for the analysis; you see it before sending and can leave it out.
- Augment mods say which Warframe they are for, e.g. "Augment (Trinity)".
- A mod's rarity sits on its rank line, so long names have room.

## 2026.11.14.460 (2026-11-14)

- Feedback from inside the app: the new Feedback button sends a bug, wish or idea as an issue on GitHub (sign in with GitHub once, in Settings or the Feedback window).
- The navigation stays in place while you scroll, with Feedback and Settings always at the bottom; Inventory and Progression fold away with a click (remembered).
- A compact profile card with your in-game name (from AlecaFrame): click it (or point at it) for the details.
- After an update, Windows says once which version you have now; the tray icon shows the version when you point at it.
- Locked pages say more precisely what to do first (Incarnon: the quests that lead there; Arcanes: every island they come from).
- Smaller fixes for updates and phone access.
