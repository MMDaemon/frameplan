# Settings

Open **Settings** at the bottom of the page's navigation. The tray menu
(right-click the icon in the taskbar's notification area) has the program's
settings too; both always agree.

## What the game hasn't opened yet
Pages, parts of a page, islands, places and planets the game opens later:
Incarnon after its quests, Arcanes once you can get one, selling on the
Surplus page at Mastery Rank 2 (trading also needs Two-Factor Authentication),
the Helminth once its segment is built, a planet behind its junction, the
Steel Path before Teshin opens it … Choose how they show: **grey them out**
(the default — with a lock and what unlocks them), **hide them** (they appear
by themselves later), or **show them as any other**.

## Items you can't get yet
Items locked by your Mastery Rank or by an island you can't play yet:
**greyed out** (the default) or **shown as any other** they count in your
goals; **hidden** they don't. The Items and Islands pages' Display switch is
the same setting.

## Appearance
Dark, light or like the browser. Each browser and device keeps its own choice
(a phone can be light while the PC is dark).

## The program
These are the tray menu's settings. On a phone they are shown but can only be
changed on the PC Frameplan runs on.

- **Updates** — install automatically, tell me when there is one, or off.
  *Check for updates* asks now; *Install* installs the new version (the program
  restarts by itself).
- **Start with Windows** — Frameplan starts quietly when you sign in.
- **Phone access (local network)** — phones and tablets in your home network
  that scan the QR code can open the page. It shows your inventory, so only
  turn it on in a network you trust.
- **Live updates** — rebuild the page when your inventory changes.
  *Refresh the page now* rebuilds it at once.
- **Notifications** — live offers of missing items (Baro, Nightwave …),
  reminders before a reset, the weekly plan for your Arcanes, page ready /
  page build failed.

## Feedback
Sign in with GitHub (on the PC) to send feedback from the app's **Feedback**
button: Frameplan shows a code, you enter it on GitHub. *Sign out* forgets the
sign-in on this PC. A phone with phone access can send too, in the name signed
in on the PC. See [Privacy](privacy.md#feedback) for what is sent.
