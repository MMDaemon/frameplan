# User guide

Frameplan's page has two kinds of pages: **Inventory** (what you have and what
is still missing) and **Progression** (where, how and what to play to get it),
with the **Overview** above both.

## Overview
Your Mastery progress, what to do now (builds that are ready, things to buy,
daily and weekly tasks), and your progress over time.

## Inventory
- **Items** — every item you still miss for Mastery or your goals, with its
  parts, what each part needs, where it drops and whether you can build it now.
- **Supplies** — things you keep a stock of (Formas, Gear, resources with a
  blueprint) with a target each: keep at least N, as many as possible, or none.
- **Mastery** — what still gives Mastery and how much.
- **Mods** — the mods you miss or could rank up, where they drop or who sells
  them, and what to sell or dissolve.
- **Arcanes** — each Arcane's rank, how many copies are still needed for max,
  and the quickest source.
- **Incarnon** — Incarnon weapons and Genesis adapters: evolutions, what's
  next, this week's Circuit rotation.
- **Trading** — what is worth selling and for how much (warframe.market prices).

## Progression
- **Islands** — the heart of the planner. An *island* is a place you farm in one
  session (the Star Chart, Zariman, Cambion Drift, Void Fissures …). Each island
  lists, place by place, everything you still miss there — items with their
  missing parts, Arcanes, resources and mods — and every drop place, the best
  marked with a star. It also says what it takes to get in (a Dragon Key for an
  Orokin Vault, a Search Pulse for a Netracell …) and how far you are.
- **Resources** — the resources you are short of, what needs them and where
  they come from.
- **Relics** — which relics hold parts you need, where to get them and which to
  open.
- **Syndicates** — your ranks and Standing, today's trade-ins, the offerings you
  need and the mods you miss there.
- **Activities** — daily and weekly tasks, ticked off as you play.

Everything links both ways: an item, Arcane, resource or mod leads to its
islands, and an island back to each of them.

## Colours and symbols
Frameplan never uses colour alone: every state also has an icon and words
(blue with a check = done, orange with a triangle = still missing, a lock =
not unlocked yet).
